How a site goes live

1 · RoxyClient approval, domain added to Cloudflare
2 · IgnacioQA the DNS records, switch nameservers
3 · Daily check (9:41 ET)Preflight, launch, verify, update sheet
4 · JudithPost-launch checks, cancel Webflow hosting

The daily check reads the control sheet and team Slack, refuses to launch anything that fails preflight (wrong content, firewall rules, unexpected DNS), rolls back on failure, and posts one summary to the team each weekday. A human approves any security or email change.

Problems caught before they reached clients

  • CaughtTemplate content on production, 2 sites
    The new site's live branch still had sample data. Preflight blocked the launch until the build was fixed.
  • FixedOld firewall rules waking up, 5 sites
    2023 rules blocking visitors outside the US became active once traffic ran through Cloudflare. Disabled; now checked before every launch.
  • FixedOld domains left behind, 3 restored
    Secondary domains that relied on Webflow broke when hosting ended. Now redirect to the new sites; one more (citydentalart.com) queued before Webflow is cancelled.
  • FixedEmail authentication errors, 2 practices
    A mail-server mismatch and a duplicate policy record, found during launch checks and corrected.
  • FoundGoogle Ads crawler errors on a WordPress client
    Ads stopped serving because the host site crashed for Google's bot. Diagnosed and handed to the hosting owner.
