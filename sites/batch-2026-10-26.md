## Flags (not caused by the cutover; check before cancelling Webflow)

1. **Subdomains still pointing at Webflow.** They break when Webflow hosting is
   cancelled, unless they're separate Webflow sites that stay:
   - sunrisesmilesid.com: `visit` → cdn.webflow.com
   - myvipdentistry.com: `visit` → cdn.webflow.com
   - bloomingsmilesdentalstudio.com: `visit` → proxy-ssl.webflow.com
   - hudsonoaksdentist.com: `visit` and `promo` (proxied) → proxy-ssl.webflow.com
   - madisondentalstudio.com: wildcard `A * 198.202.211.1` (Webflow IP)
2. **dentistingreenwoodin.com mail:** MX is `mail.dentistingreenwoodin.com`, which
   only resolves through the proxied wildcard `CNAME * → apex`, so it lands on
   Cloudflare's proxy, which can't take SMTP. That was already the case before
   the cutover. Confirm whether the practice receives mail at this domain.
3. **chesterfieldwoodlakedental.com:** a `tel:(000)000-0000` link sits in the
   "Request Appointment Or Call: (314) 370-5713" button, the same as on the old
   Webflow site. Probably a placeholder the DNI script is meant to swap. Worth a
   test call/click.
4. Unbounce `specials` subdomains (legacydentalabq, bloomingsmilesdentalstudio)
   are independent of Webflow and untouched.
